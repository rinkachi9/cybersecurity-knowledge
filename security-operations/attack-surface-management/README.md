# Attack surface management

Discovery, inventory and monitoring of the points where an unauthorized party can try to enter or extract data, seen from outside and inside.

Status: scaffold.

## Scope

- Belongs here: external and cloud attack surface discovery, exposure monitoring, dangling DNS and certificate hygiene, shadow asset detection from the outside.
- Belongs elsewhere: the internal inventory is in [asset management](../../asset-security/asset-management/README.md), the configuration of cloud accounts is in [CSPM](../../security-architecture-and-engineering/cloud-and-infrastructure-security/cloud-security-posture-management.md), and the fixing of findings is in [vulnerability remediation](../vulnerability-management/vulnerability-remediation/README.md).

## CISSP sections

1.9, 3.5, 4.1, 7.2 and 7.8 of the ISC2 CISSP Exam Outline effective April 15, 2024.
