# Digital forensics

Collecting, preserving and examining digital evidence so that findings can be trusted by an investigator, a court, a regulator or an auditor. In the CISSP outline this is section 7.1 (investigations), and it connects to incident response (section 7.6).

## Notes

- [Chain of custody](chain-of-custody/README.md): The documented record of who held evidence and what they did with it, with the order of volatility, acquisition and integrity checks, the legal context and a tested hash-chained custody log.
