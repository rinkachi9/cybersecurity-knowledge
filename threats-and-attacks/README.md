# Threats and attacks

Kinds of threats and the attack techniques behind them. Notes describe how attacks work so that defenders can recognize and stop them. Practical exercises belong in a lab, a CTF or an authorized test.

## Notes

- [Threats & attacks](threats-and-attacks-overview.md): Threat classifications, categories, impact and defenses.
- [Attack categorization](attack-categorization.md): Ways to group attacks: by vector, technique, objective and actor.
- [User-based attacks](user-based-attacks.md): Attacks that target users instead of software.

### Malware

Malicious software.

Entry point: [malware](malware/README.md).

- [Malware definition and purpose](malware/malware-definition-and-purpose.md): What malware is and what it is used for.
- [Malware](malware/malware.md): Classification, techniques, detection and prevention of malware.

### Social engineering

Attacks on people and physical access.

Entry point: [social-engineering](social-engineering/README.md).

- [Psychology of cyberattacks](social-engineering/psychology-of-cyberattacks.md): Psychological principles that attackers exploit and how to defend.
- [Social engineering](social-engineering/social-engineering.md): Manipulating people instead of exploiting technical flaws.
- [Phishing](social-engineering/phishing.md): Impersonating trusted senders to obtain information or actions.
- [Spam](social-engineering/spam.md): Mass unsolicited messages and their security impact.
- [Impersonation](social-engineering/impersonation.md): Pretending to be a trusted person, entity or role.
- [Shoulder surfing](social-engineering/shoulder-surfing.md): Watching a screen or keyboard to capture sensitive information.
- [Tailgating](social-engineering/tailgating.md): Following an authorized person into a restricted area.
- [Dumpster diving](social-engineering/dumpster-diving.md): Searching discarded material for sensitive information.

### Web attacks

Attacks that involve websites and domains.

Entry point: [web-attacks](web-attacks/README.md).

- [Web-based attacks](web-attacks/web-based-attacks.md): Attacks on web applications, servers and their users.
- [Watering Hole Attack](web-attacks/watering-hole-attack.md): Compromising a site that a target group visits.
- [Drive-By Attack](web-attacks/drive-by-attack.md): Malware delivered simply by visiting a page.
- [Typosquatting](web-attacks/typosquatting.md): Look-alike domains that exploit typing mistakes and homoglyphs.

### Credential attacks

Attacks that guess credentials.

Entry point: [credential-attacks](credential-attacks/README.md).

- [Brute Force Attack](credential-attacks/brute-force-attack.md): Trying many combinations to guess credentials or keys.
- [Password Spray](credential-attacks/password-spray.md): Trying a few common passwords against many accounts.

### Attack lifecycle

Phases that appear in the course of an attack.

Entry point: [attack-lifecycle](attack-lifecycle/README.md).

- [Reconnaissance](attack-lifecycle/reconnaissance.md): Collecting information about a target before an attack.
- [Privilege escalation](attack-lifecycle/privilege-escalation.md): Gaining higher access than originally granted.
