## Categorization

Cybersecurity attacks can be categorized in several ways, but for a practical security documentation and training perspective, they’re often grouped by:

1. **Vector-Based Attacks** - How the attack is delivered (email, web, USB, etc.).
2. **Technique-Based Attacks** - The core method used (social engineering, exploitation, brute force).
3. **Objective-Based Attacks** - What the attacker wants (data theft, account compromise, financial fraud).
4. **Actor-Based Attacks** - Who is behind the attack (cybercriminals, state actors, insiders).
