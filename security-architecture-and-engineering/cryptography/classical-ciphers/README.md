# Classical ciphers

Historical pen-and-paper ciphers, kept for the concepts they teach and for CTF and forensic recognition.

Status: scaffold.

## Scope

- Belongs here: substitution and transposition ciphers used before computers, such as the Caesar and Vigenere ciphers, and how they are broken.
- Belongs elsewhere: modern ciphers are in [encryption](../encryption/README.md), general attack classes such as frequency analysis are classified in [cryptographic attacks](../cryptographic-attacks/README.md).

## CISSP sections

3.6 and 3.7 of the ISC2 CISSP Exam Outline effective April 15, 2024.

## Notes

- [Caesar cipher](caesar-cipher/README.md): Shift substitution, brute force and chi-squared frequency analysis, with a tested script.
