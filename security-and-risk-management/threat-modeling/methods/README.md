# Methods

Individual threat modeling methods.

## Notes

- [STRIDE](stride.md): Six threat categories mapped to security properties, applied per DFD element, with a worked example.
- [Attack Trees](attack-trees.md): Representing how an attacker can reach a goal as a tree of steps.
- [Process for Attack Simulation and Threat Analysis (PASTA)](pasta.md): Seven risk-centric stages from business objectives to attack simulation and a risk heat map.
- [Trike](trike.md): Requirements-based audit method: actor-asset-action matrix, generated DoS and EoP threats, exposure scores.
- [VAST](vast.md): Visual, Agile and Simple Threat modeling: application and operational models for large portfolios.
- [Hybrid Threat Modeling Method (hTMM)](hybrid-threat-modeling-method.md): Combining several methodologies in one process.
- [Security Cards](security-cards.md): Card-based, collaborative threat discovery.
