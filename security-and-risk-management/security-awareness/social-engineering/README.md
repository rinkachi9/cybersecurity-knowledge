# Social engineering

Attacks on people and physical access.

## Notes

- [Psychology of cyberattacks](psychology-of-cyberattacks.md): Psychological principles that attackers exploit and how to defend.
- [Social engineering](social-engineering.md): Manipulating people instead of exploiting technical flaws.
- [Phishing](phishing.md): Impersonating trusted senders to obtain information or actions.
- [Angler phishing](angler-phishing.md): Fake customer support accounts on social media that answer customers' complaints, with a lookalike detector.
- [Quid pro quo](quid-pro-quo.md): Offering help or a reward in exchange for access, fake IT support and help desk attacks.
- [Spam](spam.md): Mass unsolicited messages and their security impact.
- [Impersonation](impersonation.md): Pretending to be a trusted person, entity or role.
- [Shoulder surfing](shoulder-surfing.md): Watching a screen or keyboard to capture sensitive information.
- [Tailgating](tailgating.md): Following an authorized person into a restricted area, controls and log correlation to detect it.
- [Dumpster diving](dumpster-diving.md): Searching discarded material for sensitive information.
