# Security and risk management

This CISSP domain covers security concepts, governance, law, risk, threat modeling and security awareness. It also covers business continuity requirements. The notes below follow the main purpose of each topic, with links to related domains where a subject overlaps.

## Reading order

1. [Foundations](foundations/README.md): Security concepts and the confidentiality, integrity and availability model.
2. [Governance and compliance](governance-and-compliance/README.md): Policy, risk management, control frameworks and standards.
3. [Threat modeling](threat-modeling/README.md): Methods for identifying plausible threats and design failures.
4. [Security awareness](security-awareness/README.md): Social engineering and attacks that target people.
5. [Privacy regulations](privacy-regulations/README.md): Legal and regulatory requirements for personal data.
6. [Reference](reference/README.md): Organizations and certifications used throughout this repository.
