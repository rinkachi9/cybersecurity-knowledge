# Privacy and data protection

Laws and frameworks that govern personal data, and what they require from a security program.

## Notes

- [Data protection and privacy regulations](privacy-regulations-overview.md): What privacy regulations are and how they compare.
- [General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)](gdpr.md): The European Union General Data Protection Regulation.
- [Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)](pipeda.md): Canada federal private-sector privacy law.
- [Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)](hipaa.md): U.S. law protecting health information.
- [Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Act](ftc-act.md): The Federal Trade Commission role in privacy and data security.
- [Children’s Online Privacy Protection Rule (COPPA)](coppa.md): U.S. rules on online data collection from children.
- [Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA)](glba.md): U.S. law on protecting customer financial information.
- [NIST Privacy Framework](nist-privacy-framework.md): The NIST voluntary framework for managing privacy risk.
